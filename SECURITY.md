# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Write to **privacidad@thelever.app**, in English or Spanish. Please do not open a public issue, pull
request or discussion about a vulnerability until it is fixed: this repository is public.

Tell us what you found, where, and the smallest steps that show it. We answer within 5 working
days and keep you posted until it is fixed.

## Scope

- The app and the public web at thelever.app, and the room server they talk to.
- The code in this repository.

Out of scope: denial-of-service by volume, social engineering, and the services of our providers
(Google Cloud and Firebase, Hostinger, Anthropic, Ko-fi), which you should report to them.

## Testing

Use only your own anonymous identity and rooms you create. Do not access, change or delete the data
of other players, do not degrade the service for them, and stop as soon as you have shown the
problem. If you act in good faith within these rules, we will not take legal action against you.

There is no bug bounty: TheLever is a free game run by one person. We are glad to credit you once
the fix is out, if you want.
